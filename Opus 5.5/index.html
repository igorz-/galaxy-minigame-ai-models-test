    Captain's log

      A twenty-turn duel

      Void Empires Blitz

      Lead the Aurora Concord against the Vulcan Hegemony across a twenty-world chart. Everything is public. Nothing is random in combat. Each empire gets one order per turn.

      1. 1
        Select a world on the chart. Selecting is free.
      2. 2
        Choose an order (Expand, Develop or Attack) and read its exact price and effect.
      3. 3
        Commit the order or Pass. You move first on odd turns, the rival on even turns.

      The Meridian relays

      Worlds 08 and 13 awaken at the end of turn 5 and gain +1 Economy and +1 Industry. From then through turn 20, each relay pays its holder 2 prestige at the end of every turn. Banked prestige stays banked.

      Scheduled events

      Turn 11: Supernova. No Military income.
      Turns 16–19: Blockade. Attacks cost +8 Military.
      Turn 20: Collapse. Final score.

      Victory

      Take the rival's last world, or hold more prestige after turn 20: 3 per world + every E/I/D level + relay prestige.
