Void Empires — Blitz

Twenty worlds. Two empires. Twenty shared turns. Bank prestige from the ancient relays, out-produce your rival, and end the match with the higher score — or take their last world and win on the spot.

1
Select a world

Tap any world on the chart. Selecting spends nothing — the inspector shows owner, profile, E/I/D, production, and capture protection.

2
Choose a legal order

Expand onto an adjacent neutral world, Develop one attribute for 8¢, or Attack an adjacent enemy world. Every price is shown before you commit.

3
Commit or Pass

Commit resolves your order immediately; Pass keeps your reserves. On even turns the rival moves first, after income.

◆
Relay timing: at the end of turn 5 both relays awaken (+1 Economy, +1 Industry) and each pays its owner +2 prestige. They keep paying every turn through turn 20 — holding one for all 16 awards is worth 32 prestige. Watch for the supernova (no Military income on turn 11) and the blockade (+8¢ attack cost, turns 16–19).

Mouse, touch, and keyboard supported. Keyboard: 1/2/3 stage orders · ↑↓←→ navigate · Enter commit · P pass
