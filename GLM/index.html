VOID EMPIRES — BLITZ
Lead the Solace Accord across a 20-turn galaxy. Claim worlds, raise their levels, and bank prestige from the two ancient relays before the Vex Dominion does.
1

Select a world on the star chart — every world, lane, level, and price is public. Inspecting costs nothing.

2

Stage an order: Expand, Develop, or Attack. A staged order follows your selection — click another world to retarget it, and see its exact price before committing.

3

Commit or Pass. One order per turn. You move first on odd turns; the rival moves first on even turns.

FIELD MANUAL

INCOMEeach turn: +2 Credits per Economy · +1 Military per Industry (owned worlds only)
EXPAND12 + 4 × your world count Credits → claim an adjacent neutral world, attributes intact
DEVELOP8 Credits → raise one attribute by 1 (max level 5), production from next turn
ATTACK8 Credits + 4 + 6 × target Defense Military → capture an adjacent enemy world
Captured worlds are shielded for two turns (attackable on the third).
The relays — 08 Meridian and 13 Zenith — awaken at the end of turn 5 and bank +2 prestige per held turn through turn 20.
Turn 11: a solar flare suppresses all Military income. Turns 16–19: a blockade adds +8 Military to every attack.
Final score = 3 per world + attribute levels + banked relay prestige. Higher total wins — or wipe out the rival entirely.
