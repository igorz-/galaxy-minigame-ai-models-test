    Void Empires Blitz

    Lead the Nadir Accord against the Vulcan Pact. Everything is public, nothing is random in combat, and each shared turn you get exactly one order or a pass.

    1. 1 · Select a world on the chart. Selecting spends nothing.
    2. 2 · Choose Expand, Develop or Attack and read the exact price and effect.
    3. 3 · Commit the order, or Pass. The turn then resolves.
    • Economy pays 2 Credits per level each turn; Industry pays 1 Military; Defense makes a world costlier to take.
    • Relays (08 and 13) awaken at the end of turn 5 (+1 Economy, +1 Industry) and bank +2 prestige per turn for their owner from turn 5 to 20.
    • Turn 11: no Military income. Turns 16–19: attacks cost +8 Military. Captured worlds are shielded for one extra turn.
    • Highest prestige after turn 20 wins: 3 per world + all attributes + banked relay prestige. Capturing every rival world wins at once.
