    A pocket war for one sector

    Void Empires · Blitz

    You and a rival power awake on opposite ends of a twenty-world sector. Twenty shared turns. One order each per turn. No dice — every price and every lane is on the chart.

    1
    Select a world

    Tap any planet. The inspector shows its owner, attributes and production.

    2
    Stage an order

    Expand onto a linked unclaimed world, Develop one attribute of a world you hold, or Attack a linked enemy world. Illegal options explain exactly why.

    3
    Commit — or pass

    One order per turn, then your rival answers. On odd turns you act first; on even turns it does.

    The ancient relays. The two gold-ringed worlds (08 & 13) are relics worth 2 prestige each to their holder at the end of every turn from 5 onward — 32 over a full game. They awaken at the end of turn 5, gaining +1 Economy and +1 Industry. Win by eliminating the rival, or by the higher prestige when the galaxy is scored after turn 20.
    Marked on the calendar. Turn 11: a supernova suppresses all Military income for both sides. Turns 16–19: a blockade adds +8 Military to every attack.

    Hotkeys: 1 Expand · 2 Develop · 3 Attack · P Pass
