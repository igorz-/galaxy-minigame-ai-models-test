    Void Empires Blitz

    A dying galaxy. Twenty turns. Two empires. The stars themselves are keeping score.

    1. Select a world on the star chart — every world, lane, and reserve is public.
    2. Choose an order — Expand, Develop, or Attack — and read its exact price and effect.
    3. Commit or Pass. One order per turn; on even turns the rival moves first.

    The two golden relays awaken at the end of turn 5, then bank 2 prestige every turn for whoever holds them. Hold one from awakening to collapse for 32 prestige — often the whole match.
