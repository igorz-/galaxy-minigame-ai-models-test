Make them count.

An open galaxy. A predictable rival. Every decision is yours.

01

Select a world

All 20 worlds are visible. Lanes determine where you can expand or attack.

02

Choose an order

Expand your borders, develop a world, or attack the rival. Inspect exact costs first.

03

Commit, or pass

One order each per shared turn. You go first on odd turns; the rival goes first on even turns.

Build an engine. Every turn, each world generates 2 × Economy in Credits and 1 × Industry in Military. Develop any attribute for 8 Credits, up to level 5. Expansion costs 12 + 4 per world you already own.

Take your chances. Without chance. An attack costs 8 Credits and 4 + 6 × target Defense in Military. Captures preserve attributes and protect the world through the next turn. Losing your home is not defeat; losing your final world is.

Own the middle. The two relays awaken at the end of turn 5, gaining +1 Economy and Industry (max 5). Each held relay banks 2 prestige at the end of turns 5–20.

Watch the horizon. Turn 11 brings no Military income. Attacks cost +8 Military on turns 16–19 only. Nothing is hidden and combat has no dice rolls.

Leave a legacy. At the end of turn 20: 3 per world + all attribute levels + banked relay prestige. Credits and Military do not score. Equal prestige is a draw.
